# Ultras

How a party of accounts beats AQW's ultra bosses with the Ultras-v3 Scripts, and how strategies for them are written down and tested.

## Language

### Strategy

**Ultra**:
A hard boss fought by a party of 4 accounts, whose quest can be completed once a day or once a week.
_Avoid_: raid, ultra boss fight

**Comp**:
A named strategy for one Ultra: its 4 classes, each class's Loadout, and when each class taunts. The developer designs Comps; agents don't.
_Avoid_: team, group, setup, build

**Party Layout**:
Which account plays which class of a Comp. It names real accounts, so it stays on the developer's machine.
_Avoid_: roster, class assignment

**Loadout**:
What one class in a Comp brings: its class, weapon, cape and helm enhancements, and its potions and scroll.
_Avoid_: kit, build, gear set

**Role**:
The job a class has in a Comp, such as taunting the left orb or hitting the boss.
_Avoid_: slot, position

### Testing

**Attempt**:
One fight with a Comp, from engaging the boss to its kill or the whole party's death.
_Avoid_: try, run, pull

**Wipe**:
An Attempt that ends with the whole party dead.
_Avoid_: fail, reset
